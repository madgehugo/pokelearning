Pokélearning: learn German while you play Pokémon
==================================================

1. Drag Pokélearning into the Applications folder.

2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the
   app for malicious software, because it isn't from the App Store or signed
   with a paid Apple developer account. To open it anyway:
     - Click "Done" (or "OK") in that message.
     - Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to
       "Pokélearning was blocked…", and click "Open Anyway".
     - Confirm with "Open Anyway" and your password.
   You only do this once.

3. Choose your game: the .gba file of your Pokémon game, dumped from your own
   cartridge (for example Pokémon Smaragd). No games are included.

4. The first launch downloads a German dictionary, game names and a
   translation model (about 230 MB, a few minutes). After that it works
   without an internet connection.

From then on, open Pokélearning to play: the game starts in mGBA, and a panel
next to it shows every German line with its translation and its words.
Review, your words and the games are in the Go menu.

Your progress is kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Pokélearning.
File → Back Up Progress… saves it to one file, for example to move to
another Mac (File → Restore Progress… there).
